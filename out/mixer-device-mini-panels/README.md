# Mixer: Device Mini Panels

Shows each device's remote-control knobs right under its name in the mixer's **Devices** section, so EQ+, Compressor and other native devices can be adjusted from every mixer channel.

- Turn on the mixer's Devices section (the section toggles at the bottom of the mixer).
- Each device that has remote-control pages gets a mini panel with the knobs of its current page. Native devices come with pages; plug-ins show one once you map remote controls for them.
- Change the page in the device panel and the mini panel follows.
- The knobs are Bitwig's own, so automation, modulation and undo behave as usual.
