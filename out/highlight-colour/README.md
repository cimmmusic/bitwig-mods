# Interface: Highlight Colour

Changes Bitwig's highlight colour: the orange of selected buttons and options, values, the playing transport, LEDs, drop indicators, beat markers and notifications.

## Settings

In **Settings > Mods > Highlight Colour**:

- **Highlight colour**: *Theme colour* (unchanged), a preset (Orange, Red, Pink, Purple, Violet, Blue, Cyan, Mint, Green, Lime, Yellow, White) or *Custom hue*.
- **Custom hue (°)**: the colour wheel position for *Custom hue*: 0 red, 30 orange, 60 yellow, 120 green, 180 cyan, 220 blue, 280 purple, 330 pink.

Changes apply at once. Each highlight keeps its own brightness and transparency (the dimmer "subtle" highlight stays dimmer), and colours your theme sets to something else (white knob values in Minimal, for example) are left alone. Track and clip colours are never changed.

Works with any theme, with or without Framework: Themes.
