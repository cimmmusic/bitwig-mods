# Minimal

A near-black, flat theme for Bitwig Studio.

- No gradients: every fill is a single colour.
- No backgrounds or outlines behind buttons (play, record, toolbar and view buttons): just their icons, with a soft grey when selected or pressed.
- No shadows and no embossing on knobs.
- No little corner arrows on menu buttons (FILE, PLAY, ADD...): an empty icon replaces Bitwig's.
- With Interface: Pill Buttons, the toolbar buttons (play, stop, record...) have no outlines either.
- Hairline separators, white text and icons, one orange accent for what's on or playing.
- Track and clip colours are left as they are.

Needs Framework: Themes. Choose **Minimal** in its Themes tab in BML, then restart Bitwig.
