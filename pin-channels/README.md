# Pin Channels

Keep chosen channels in view while you scroll: handy for VCAs, buses or a reference track.

## Pinning

- Right-click a track (or several selected tracks) and choose Pin Channel. Unpin Channel takes it out again.
- Click the pin icon on a pinned channel to unpin it.
- Pins are remembered between sessions.

## Arranger

- Pinned channels sit in a fixed bar at the top of the arranger, one row each: pin, name, record arm, solo, mute and a volume fader.
- The bar never scrolls. The channels' own rows and clip lanes stay where they are in the track list.

## Mixer

- Pinned channels sit in a fixed area at the left of the mixer, one strip each, with the same controls.

## Controls

- Click R, S or M to toggle record arm, solo or mute.
- Drag the fader to change the volume; double-click it for 0 dB. Each change is one undo step.
